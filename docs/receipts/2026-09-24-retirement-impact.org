* Seven retirement checks

1. The old graph retains the historical result, active job, queued job and its
   verification dependency; an unrelated export is excluded. The verification
   witness is =automatic-retry → queued-job → verify-queued-retry=.
2. Selecting the removed identity in the candidate graph raises an error
   instead of reporting no impact; the before graph remains available.
3. An incomplete external-consumer inventory yields =partial-impact=.
4. The cut at logical position 4 separates past completion/start, explicit
   current activity, declared future retry, counterfactual retirement and an
   out-of-horizon retry. No activity is inferred merely from an old start.
5. Creating the later cut retains the earlier historical cut and its original
   observed result/payload.
6. A missing causal parent remains =unknown-consumer-origin= in the unknown
   frontier and yields partial temporal classification.
7. The retirement proposal cannot be selected as an observed trigger in the
   current cut. Neither structural nor temporal analysis grants execution.

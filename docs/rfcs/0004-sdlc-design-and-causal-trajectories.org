* Decision summary and scope

The complete Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is Aitia's organizing
framework. Requirements, engineering design, algorithm and implementation
strategies, verification and testing, delivery, operation, maintenance and
retirement all participate in that lifecycle. Causal Assurance maintains the
justification of engineering conclusions as these activities change the system.

Software design receives early engineering investment because cheaper code
generation increases the value of deciding what to build, under which
assumptions, with which responsibilities and guarantees. This is a product
direction to evaluate through qualification cases, not a claim that code cost
or design quality has already been measured.

The eight-step =change -> invalidate -> plan -> verify -> admit -> decide ->
authorize -> effect= flow is the existing assurance/execution protocol. It can
be used at multiple lifecycle points; it is not an enumeration of the entire
SDLC. The overall lifecycle contains feedback, concurrent activities and
repeated revisions. A bounded execution plan may be a DAG without turning the
whole lifecycle into one fixed pipeline.

This RFC records the agreed direction and pending executable acceptance work.
Its draft status does not promote the capabilities below to implemented APIs.

The lifecycle workflow below names engineering states, not implementation
milestones. Transitions require attributable revisions and receipts; an arrow
does not itself grant assurance or authority. Feedback can reopen earlier
activities, and independent obligations may proceed concurrently.

These states summarize activity, not a single global status enum. Multiple
claims may have different support states at the same cut. Pending or stale
support blocks the affected effect; an accepted ADR alone is not authorization.
Operational findings or changed context trigger reassessment; an accountable
retirement decision opens retirement obligations before closure is recorded.

* Design and implementation within the lifecycle

| Activity | Engineering question | Attributable output |
|----------+----------------------+---------------------|
| Requirements and context | What outcome is needed, for whom and under what constraints? | revisioned requirements, scope and acceptance conditions |
| Software and engineering design | Who owns state, responsibility, interfaces, failure handling and effects? | alternatives, assumptions, guarantees and RFC/ADR decisions |
| Algorithms and implementation strategies | How can the chosen guarantees be realized within cost and resource constraints? | strategy choice, implementation mapping and measurements |
| Verification and testing | Which precise propositions have supporting or contradicting evidence? | scoped proofs, model checks, tests, replay and review |
| Delivery and operation | Which action is currently permitted, and what actually happened? | authority, execution receipts and observed trajectories |
| Maintenance and retirement | Which assumptions changed, and what must be revised, migrated or stopped? | invalidation, new obligations and accountable successor decisions |

Activities share one lifecycle subject and revision history. An algorithm
change that alters consistency or failure guarantees is also a design
decision. A performance optimization within the same contract still needs
conformance and performance evidence. Estimated cost, human judgment and
measured behavior retain distinct evidence kinds and scopes.

Design work relates existing POO values through:

    G --> I["Implementation strategy<br/>candidate"]

    E --> C{"Scoped admission and assessment"}

    X --> O

A guarantee states its subject, predicate and scope under named assumptions.
An assumption states a premise, its owner and the conditions requiring review.
An ADR records considered options and their consequences; acceptance records
the design choice, while current assurance requires separately admitted
evidence. Relaxing a requirement is an explicit requirement revision and
review, not a repair technique that silently turns a failed check green.

The existing =SdlcDesignContract= supplies responsibilities, owned state,
allowed effects, assumptions, guarantees, variations and a decision identity.
Its replacement review admits exact declaration comparisons only. Logical
implication between different statements, model/code correspondence and
cross-component guarantees require additional obligations with attributable
checks. Neither name equality nor two successful component checks establishes
the safety of their composition.

* System behavior and design evolution trajectories

POO Flow owns Temporal Causality, event graphs, causal cuts, trajectory
contracts, witness paths and traversal. Aitia supplies software event meaning
and the links from behavior to requirements, assumptions, design decisions and
assurance. Python transports observations and invokes tools through the owned
runtime boundary.

Two related trajectories are required:

- system behavior: requests, state transitions, retries, effects, failures and
  recovery in an explicitly identified environment;
- design and assurance evolution: hypotheses, RFC/ADR choices, checks,
  findings, invalidation, revision and renewed acceptance.

Evidence and checked projections connect these trajectories. A runtime event
can challenge an assumption; a reviewed explanation can motivate an ADR
revision. Structural impact, temporal precedence and graph reachability do not
by themselves establish a causal explanation. Agent-proposed explanations
remain hypotheses until their supporting checks are admitted.

The cross-trajectory links are evidence and obligation relations, not an
automatic causal inference from event order. A proposed ADR retains its
assumptions even when the explanation remains unresolved.

| Trajectory source | Scope that must remain visible |
|-------------------+--------------------------------|
| Production or experimental execution | observed events in the named system or harness, with source and environment bindings |
| TLC/model-check counterexample | behavior permitted by the exact model and checked configuration; implementation correspondence is a separate obligation |
| Proposed intervention or alternative design | counterfactual branch under explicit assumptions and a bounded horizon |
| RFC/ADR and assurance evolution | recorded choices and changes in justification, without rewriting earlier facts or granting runtime authority |

An observed failure remains observed in its original environment. The current
POO Flow trajectory contract reserves =error-event-paths= for uncommitted
counterfactuals; actual bug replays must not be relabeled to fit that slot.
Use the existing observed event graph, and place any missing generic trajectory
mechanism in its POO Flow owner before exposing an Aitia projection.

An assessment binds the event graph, immutable causal cut, explicit time
observation, source/design/policy revisions and horizon. Occurrence order,
ingress order and revision order must not be conflated. A late observation
creates a new assessment cut and may invalidate current support while leaving
the earlier cut intact. Missing causal parents, incompatible clock domains
and incomplete coverage retain an unknown frontier. Cross-clock mapping or
additional time axes require a qualified POO Flow contract; this RFC does not
claim that every such mapping already exists.

* Historical engineering cases as design inputs

Issues, design discussions, incident reports and engineering articles can
contribute before implementation begins. Preserve the reasoning that connects
context, requirements, assumptions, alternatives and consequences, rather than
only a summary or a recommended pattern. Include successful designs, rejected
proposals and withdrawn fixes as well as failures.

| Use | Contribution | Admission boundary |
|-----+--------------+--------------------|
| Inspiration | proposes a question, assumption, alternative or failure scenario | similarity does not establish applicability or a local defect |
| Judgment | supports an accountable choice under explicit conditions and tradeoffs | record matching conditions, differences, counterevidence, owner and review triggers |
| Verification asset | supplies a model, checker, harness or regression | qualify the exact asset and proposition before admitting support for the current subject |

These are uses, not automatic stages of increasing trust. A useful article
need not contain a replay program. A human cost or maintainability judgment
need not become a mechanical theorem, but must retain attribution, scope and
reconsideration conditions. Neither judgment nor author reputation grants an
effect capability.

The reusable case records context/version/workload, intended guarantee,
explicit and inferred assumptions, reported events, proposed explanations,
considered alternatives, consequences and transfer conditions. Every extracted
claim points to its source passage or comment and available revision. Keep
original URL, author, publication/update and retrieval times; record digest and
reuse permissions when retaining source bytes or executable assets. A mutable
URL is not a pinned artifact. This phase references sources and records our
analysis; it does not vendor articles or assume all public material is licensed
for code/data redistribution.

Before applying a case, compare state/effect ownership, failure model,
identity, timing/retention, workload and recovery requirements against the
current design. Record the correspondence as a reviewable proposal with
supporting and contradicting observations. Search similarity, citation count,
Issue closure and Agent confidence cannot discharge that applicability review.
Multiple articles repeating the same incident are not independent evidence.

An external report is evidence of what its author reports, not a local observed
execution. Separate the system-event timeline from the history of what became
known: publication, correction, retrieval and design review. A later explanation
creates a new assessment and can reopen a decision; it does not rewrite the
earlier knowledge cut. Unknown timestamps remain unknown. Do not give a whole
case one =verified= flag: a reported failure, hypothesized cause, tested repair
and unreviewed transfer can coexist.

Use existing Artifact, Claim, Assumption, Finding, Evidence, Decision and
Obligation projections and POO composition. These conceptual correspondences
are not new implemented constructors or permission to add arbitrary edge names.
POO Flow retains generic temporal/causal and query ownership. No parallel case
graph engine, universal crawler, confidence scorer or MRR integration is
required to qualify the first case.

** First application and mechanism reuse

[[file:../adrs/0008-stable-operation-key-after-lost-ack.org][ADR 0008]] applies this method to logical-operation identity. The AWS idempotency
article supplies design questions, not evidence that Aitia or the fixture meets
the AWS contract. The source/coverage matrix in that ADR distinguishes the
existing replay from untested parameter, retention and crash boundaries.

Authorization conditions belong in the existing POO Flow Cedar Provider, using
trusted context supplied by the Host; do not create an Aitia policy evaluator.
Cedar decides whether an action is allowed for the supplied principal, resource
and context. Runtime must enforce that decision at use. Provider-side atomic
deduplication and durable effects remain execution obligations: two concurrent
requests can both be allowed without an execution-time uniqueness mechanism.
An =Allow= does not establish that a previous attempt failed or did not commit.

The current =assurance-host-cedar-request= and its currentness predicate are
preflight projections, not a connected Runtime gate. Preserve their owner and
reuse the existing effect-token machinery; qualify the missing handoff instead
of treating the whole authorization design as absent. An operation identity
survives retries, while a new attempt still requires the applicable current
authorization. Outcome-unknown retries require provider reconciliation and
idempotency guarantees, not an invented success or automatic fresh commit.

* Reuse existing verification methods and engineering assets

A Recipe is a composition of existing POO objects and slots describing
applicability, bound inputs, operations, required checks, expected artifacts
and failure handling. It is not a new configuration language or a separate
workflow engine. RFC 0003 governs its maintained and advanced expression paths.

Reuse existing functions, packages, scripts, models, harnesses and tool
commands at a boundary that preserves their correctness conditions. The reuse
unit includes the checking implementation, necessary dependencies, real
execution path and relevant regression tests. When those pieces are coupled,
first qualify the existing boundary or contribute a focused upstream change.
Add a new implementation only for an identified capability gap.

For every selected component or asset, record exact source revision/digest,
original attribution and license, applicability assumptions, dependencies and
the check establishing that it works in Aitia. The integration inventory must
distinguish candidates from qualified reuse. Package layout alone does not
establish a stable SDK or independent checker entry point.

TLAPS-Bench is a candidate source of proof tasks and checking components.
Retain the target statement, trusted inputs and required checks when qualifying
an entry point. Use TLAPS to check TLA+ proofs; a Lean translation is not a
prerequisite. TLC model checking and TLAPS proof checking retain their distinct
propositions. Existing proof evidence kinds can name different producers
without adding a parallel semantic schema for each tool.

Specula and its case-study archive are candidate sources of verification tools,
models, harnesses, traces and reproductions. The archive stores experiment
records; each selected record needs a replay and attribution audit. The full
Specula application is optional, not the required workflow owner. Calling an
upstream script or bounded tool command is a legitimate reuse choice.
Specula already provides continuous and incremental verification, so Aitia's
proposed value must be demonstrated through design decisions, composition
obligations and changing justification rather than that feature alone.

* Runtime ownership and future MRR work

The production Aitia Python Runtime under =bindings/python= serves all Aitia
capabilities across the SDLC. AnyIO is its asynchronous foundation for task
lifetime, cancellation, bounded concurrency and I/O. POO Flow continues to own
generic Graph/FunFlow execution and temporal/causal algorithms. Scheme/POO owns
Aitia's semantic composition, and the C ABI carries typed projections.
Native session thread affinity must be preserved at asynchronous boundaries.
Declaring the AnyIO dependency does not establish async execution or Host
admission of a tool observation.

MRR is deferred beyond the current Python implementation stage. A later design
will evaluate relation queries, derivation and lineage over lifecycle
snapshots; its role is broader than storing trace logs. Preserve identities,
revisions, typed relations, modalities, cuts, witnesses and unknown frontiers
now so a later integration can project them without redefining Aitia meaning.
Do not add an MRR dependency, query adapter, storage framework or new Python
inference engine in this phase. Future query results must retain snapshot and
lineage bindings and pass the applicable admission boundary before use.

* Qualification sequence and observable value

The primary deliverable is one connected lifecycle experiment. The current
job-executor case is a known-fault seed: per-key deduplication can succeed
while a logical operation commits twice after acknowledgement loss. Its
existing ADR 0008 and design-contract tests supply a starting point, not full
causal or Host closure.

1. Bind requirements for completion and unique logical effects, assumptions,
   source, model when available, and at least two design alternatives.
2. Replay the known failure through existing tools and preserve the actual
   experimental trajectory. Record the proposed explanation and check its
   relationship to the original per-key guarantee.
3. Compare disabling retries with preserving a stable operation identity;
   completion and provider retention assumptions remain explicit.
4. Record the chosen revision and derive its obligations. Run the appropriate
   reused tools, establish required source/model correspondence and consume
   actual Host admission where a supported conclusion is claimed.
5. Change a retention or retry assumption, or admit a late observation. Show
   the new cut, affected guarantees, explanation paths and renewed obligations.
6. Apply an implementation optimization and check both contract preservation
   and measured benefit under the same workload and environment scope.

Qualify one small TLAPS-Bench task and one sufficiently complete upstream bug
case as reusable methods. These are separate execution qualifications until
their claims actually refer to the same subject: a GCD proof cannot discharge
a job-executor guarantee. A known-answer replay is regression evidence; novel
discovery needs a separate evaluation with unexposed answers.

* Pending acceptance gates

- [ ] One historical case records source attribution, applicability and
  counterevidence, the design judgment it changed, and the resulting obligation.
  Demonstrate a source correction reopening review without overwriting history.
- [ ] ADR 0008's owner/coverage matrix is qualified, including the existing
  Cedar/Runtime path and provider-side parameter, retention and crash boundaries;
  documentation of a gap is not its executable closure.
- [ ] Upstream reuse inventory binds selected files, revisions, dependencies,
  licenses, required checks and replay evidence.
- [ ] A maintained POO Composition selects the qualified Recipe and an
  advanced override preserves mandatory checks or opens new obligations.
- [ ] The connected lifecycle case compares design alternatives against the
  same requirements, including safety and progress where applicable.
- [ ] System, model, counterfactual and design trajectories preserve scope;
  missing parents and late evidence have explicit negative cases.
- [ ] A changed assumption yields attributable invalidation witnesses and
  prevents stale support from being reused.
- [ ] Frozen Python tool observations reach the actual Scheme Host admission
  path; serialized success cannot substitute for that path.
- [ ] A contract-preserving optimization has conformance and performance
  evidence, with no unreviewed weakening of the target requirement.
- [ ] Compare the same upstream tools/assets with and without Aitia's design
  and assurance links: check missed obligations, unnecessary reruns and the
  ability to explain why a conclusion changed. Record limitations and costs.

The upstream entry points, connected trajectory projection and Host bridge
remain unqualified by this documentation change. Existing bounded primitives
and their receipts retain their existing scope.
